Hello everyone, my name is Qurriel.
The title of my poster is “Using Big Data to Protect Disaster-Affected Communities in the U.S.”

The focus of my project is about how big data can help protect people and communities during natural disasters in the United States. I want to show how data can be used to find disaster risks, send early warnings, and help emergency workers respond faster.

Natural disasters in the U.S. are becoming more serious because of climate change and extreme weather. Disasters such as floods, hurricanes, wildfires, and storms can damage homes, roads, schools, hospitals, and other public services. These disasters can affect everyone, but some groups are more at risk. For example, low-income families, elderly people, and people living in dangerous areas may have fewer resources to prepare or recover.

Because disasters can happen very quickly, early warning and fast response are very important. This is where big data can help. Big data means collecting and studying large amounts of information. This information can come from weather reports, satellite images, sensors, emergency calls, and community reports. By using this data, we can better understand where disasters may happen and which communities may need help first.

My proposed action is to build a big data-based disaster warning system by 2030. This system would collect information from satellites, weather data, sensors, and emergency reports. Then it would use this information to find high-risk areas before disasters become worse.

For example, if there is a high risk of flooding, hurricanes, or wildfires, the system could send early warnings to people in those areas. These warnings could give people more time to prepare, leave dangerous areas, or contact emergency services.

The main stakeholders for this plan would include the U.S. government, FEMA, local emergency services, technology companies, data scientists, and community organizations. Each group would have an important role. The government and FEMA could support the system. Emergency services could use the information to respond faster. Technology companies and data scientists could help build and improve the system. Community organizations could help make sure vulnerable people receive warnings and support.

This system could have many benefits. It could help predict disaster risks earlier, reduce emergency response time, protect vulnerable communities, and improve planning before and after disasters.

To measure whether this plan works, we could compare emergency response times before and after using the system. We could also check how accurate the warnings are, how many people receive the warnings, and whether injuries, deaths, and damage are reduced after disasters.

For my figures, I included two charts about natural disasters in the United States. The first figure shows the number of billion-dollar weather and climate disaster events from 1980 to 2024. The second figure shows total disaster declarations from 1995 to 2024.

These figures are important because they show that natural disasters are a serious and growing problem in the U.S. They also support my topic because they explain why better warning systems and faster emergency response are needed. If disasters are increasing, then we need better ways to protect people, especially vulnerable communities.

In conclusion, natural disasters are a major problem in the United States. They can cause serious damage and put many people in danger. However, big data can help us prepare better. It can help predict risks, send early warnings, and improve emergency response.

The main message I want the audience to remember is this: technology and data are not only for research. They can also be used to protect real people and communities before, during, and after disasters.

Thank you.

大家好，我叫 Qurriel。
我的海报题目是 “利用大数据保护美国受灾社区”。

我这个项目的重点是探讨大数据如何帮助美国的社区应对自然灾害。具体来说，我想说明大数据可以帮助我们发现灾害风险、发送提前预警，并帮助救援人员更快地做出反应。

近些年，美国的自然灾害变得越来越严重。这和气候变化以及极端天气有很大关系。比如洪水、飓风、野火和暴风雨，都会破坏房屋、道路、学校、医院和其他公共服务设施。

这些灾害会影响很多人，但有些群体会更加危险。比如低收入家庭、老年人，以及住在高风险地区的人。他们可能没有足够的资源提前准备，也可能在灾后更难恢复。

因为自然灾害经常发生得很快，所以提前预警和快速反应非常重要。这时候，大数据就可以发挥作用。大数据的意思是收集和分析大量信息。这些信息可以来自天气报告、卫星图片、传感器、紧急电话和社区报告。

通过分析这些数据，我们可以更早知道哪些地方可能会发生灾害，也可以知道哪些社区最需要帮助。

我的行动计划是，到2030年建立一个基于大数据的灾害预警系统。这个系统会收集卫星图片、天气数据、传感器信息和紧急报告。然后，它可以分析这些信息，找出高风险地区。

比如，如果某个地区可能发生洪水、飓风或野火，系统就可以提前向当地居民发送警报。这样，人们就有更多时间准备、撤离危险地区，或者联系紧急服务。

这个计划的主要参与者包括美国政府、FEMA、地方紧急救援部门、科技公司、数据科学家和社区组织。每个参与者都有重要作用。政府和 FEMA 可以提供支持。紧急救援部门可以利用数据更快行动。科技公司和数据科学家可以帮助建设和改进系统。社区组织可以帮助确保弱势群体也能收到预警和帮助。

这个系统可以带来很多好处。它可以更早预测灾害风险，减少救援反应时间，保护弱势社区，也可以帮助灾前和灾后的规划。

为了判断这个系统是否成功，我们可以比较使用系统前后的救援反应时间。我们也可以查看预警是否准确，有多少人收到了预警，以及灾后的受伤人数、死亡人数和财产损失有没有减少。

在我的海报中，我放了两个关于美国自然灾害的数据图表。第一个图表显示了1980年到2024年美国造成十亿美元以上损失的天气和气候灾害事件数量。第二个图表显示了1995年到2024年的灾害声明总数。

这两个图表很重要，因为它们说明自然灾害在美国是一个严重的问题，而且这个问题正在变得更加明显。这也支持了我的主题：我们需要更好的预警系统和更快的应急反应，尤其是为了保护弱势社区。

总的来说，自然灾害是美国面临的一个重要问题。它们会造成严重破坏，也会让很多人处于危险之中。但是，大数据可以帮助我们更好地准备和应对灾害。它可以帮助预测风险、发送提前预警，并改善紧急救援。

我希望大家记住的主要信息是：科技和数据不只是用来做研究的，它们也可以用来保护真实的人和社区，在灾害发生之前、发生期间和发生之后提供帮助。

谢谢大家。
