长夜伴浪破晓梦，梦晓破浪伴夜长

English Presentation Script

Good morning everyone. Today, my research topic is “Using Big Data to Protect Disaster-Affected Communities in the United States.”

Natural disasters are a serious problem in the United States. Disasters such as hurricanes, floods, wildfires, tornadoes, winter storms, and earthquakes can damage homes, roads, schools, hospitals, and power systems. They can also put people’s lives at risk. Some communities are more vulnerable than others because they may have fewer resources, weaker buildings, or slower access to emergency services.

The main cause of this problem is that extreme weather events are becoming more serious and more frequent. Climate change can make storms stronger, wildfires larger, and floods more dangerous. The effect is that many people may lose their homes, jobs, or access to clean water and medical help. This is why better disaster planning is important.

My research focus is: How can big data help protect disaster-affected communities in the United States?

Big data means very large amounts of information. In disaster management, this information can come from weather forecasts, satellite images, maps, sensors, emergency calls, social media posts, and government reports. When this data is collected and analyzed quickly, it can help people understand where a disaster may happen, who is most at risk, and what kind of help is needed.

Now I will explain the first figure on my poster. Figure 1 shows the average emergency response time with and without big data support. The chart compares traditional emergency response with big data-supported response. In the figure, the big data-supported response is faster for different disaster types, such as hurricanes, floods, wildfires, tornadoes, winter storms, and earthquakes. This shows that big data can help emergency teams make decisions more quickly and send help faster.

The second figure is from NOAA. It shows United States billion-dollar disaster events from 1980 to 2024. This chart shows that many disasters in the United States have caused very high economic losses. It also shows different types of disasters, such as severe storms, tropical cyclones, flooding, drought, winter storms, wildfires, and freezes. This figure is important because it shows that disasters are not only dangerous, but also very expensive. Because of this, the United States needs better ways to prepare for and respond to disasters.

My proposed action is to build a big data-based disaster warning system by 2030. This system would use satellite images, weather data, local sensors, and emergency reports. It would send early warnings to high-risk communities before disasters become more dangerous.

The main stakeholders would be the U.S. government, FEMA, local emergency services, technology companies, data scientists, and community organizations. These groups need to work together because disaster protection is not only a technology problem. It is also a community problem.

This action would help meet my objective because it could predict disaster risks earlier, reduce emergency response time, and protect vulnerable communities. It could also help emergency workers know where to send food, water, medical support, and rescue teams.

To measure success, we can compare emergency response times before and after using the system. We can also track warning accuracy, the number of people who receive early warnings, and the number of injuries, deaths, and damages after disasters.

In conclusion, natural disasters in the United States are a growing problem. Big data cannot stop disasters from happening, but it can help communities prepare earlier, respond faster, and reduce damage. My main take-away is that big data can protect disaster-affected communities by improving prediction, warning, and emergency response.

Thank you for listening.

中文翻译

大家早上好。今天我的研究主题是：“使用大数据保护美国受灾社区。”

自然灾害是美国一个严重的问题。飓风、洪水、野火、龙卷风、冬季风暴和地震等灾害会破坏房屋、道路、学校、医院和电力系统。它们也会威胁人们的生命安全。有些社区比其他社区更脆弱，因为它们可能资源较少、建筑更弱，或者获得紧急服务的速度更慢。

这个问题的主要原因是极端天气事件变得更加严重，也更加频繁。气候变化可能会让风暴更强、野火更大、洪水更危险。结果是，很多人可能会失去家园、工作，或者无法获得干净的水和医疗帮助。因此，更好的灾害规划非常重要。

我的研究重点是：大数据如何帮助保护美国受灾社区？

大数据指的是非常大量的信息。在灾害管理中，这些信息可以来自天气预报、卫星图像、地图、传感器、紧急电话、社交媒体帖子和政府报告。当这些数据被快速收集和分析时，它可以帮助人们了解灾害可能在哪里发生、谁最容易受到影响，以及需要什么样的帮助。

现在我来解释海报上的第一张图。图一展示了有大数据支持和没有大数据支持时的平均应急响应时间。 这张图比较了传统应急响应和大数据支持的应急响应。图中显示，在飓风、洪水、野火、龙卷风、冬季风暴和地震等不同灾害类型中，大数据支持的响应速度更快。这说明大数据可以帮助应急团队更快做决定，并更快派出救援。

第二张图来自 NOAA。它展示了 1980 年到 2024 年美国造成超过 10 亿美元损失的灾害事件。 这张图显示，美国很多灾害造成了非常高的经济损失。它也展示了不同类型的灾害，比如强风暴、热带气旋、洪水、干旱、冬季风暴、野火和冰冻灾害。这张图很重要，因为它说明灾害不仅危险，而且经济损失也很大。因此，美国需要更好的方式来准备和应对灾害。

我的提议行动是：到 2030 年建立一个基于大数据的灾害预警系统。 这个系统会使用卫星图像、天气数据、当地传感器和紧急报告。它可以在灾害变得更危险之前，向高风险社区发送早期预警。

主要的利益相关者包括美国政府、FEMA、当地应急服务部门、科技公司、数据科学家和社区组织。这些群体需要一起合作，因为灾害保护不仅是一个技术问题，也是一个社区问题。

这个行动可以帮助实现我的目标，因为它能更早预测灾害风险，缩短应急响应时间，并保护脆弱社区。它还可以帮助应急人员知道应该把食物、水、医疗支持和救援队送到哪里。

衡量成功的方法包括：比较使用这个系统前后的应急响应时间；追踪预警准确率；记录收到早期预警的人数；以及查看灾害后的伤亡人数和损失情况。

总之，美国的自然灾害问题正在加重。大数据不能阻止灾害发生，但它可以帮助社区更早准备、更快响应，并减少损失。我的核心结论是：大数据可以通过改善预测、预警和应急响应来保护受灾社区。

谢谢大家聆听。
